# Douglas C. Montgomery: publicly available teaching materials

Checked November 6, 2026. This collection covers the four substantive ASU course topics in the [saved faculty profile](../../Douglas%20Montgomery%20_%20ASU%20Search.html) and the most relevant official textbook companions.

**There is substantial public supporting material, but this search did not find a complete open release of his ASU courses.** We collected **88 downloadable resource files and two source-page snapshots**, approximately **84.7 MB** altogether. The collection also indexes 19 publisher-hosted time-series videos, nine ASU-listed Fireside Chat recordings, a Montgomery/Jones webinar, and two introductory videos. These counts describe this collection, not the percentage of all his teaching material that is public.

## Videos and software examples

- **19 short time-series videos, totaling 2:31:56:** publicly accessible [Wiley video gallery](https://higheredbcs.wiley.com/legacy/college/montgomery/139418669X/vids/9781394186693_Videos.html). The [video inventory](research/time-series-videos.json) records titles, IDs, and durations; a [gallery snapshot](snapshots/time-series-3e-video-gallery.html) is saved locally. The player disables downloads, so video files were not copied. The public metadata does not identify the speaker; these are not attributed to Montgomery personally.
- **Nine Fireside Chat recordings:** official ASU links to guest presentations in Montgomery's series. Topics include screening designs, foldovers, cross-validation, space-filling designs, and software testing. See the [video index](VIDEOS.md) and [ASU source](https://asuengineeringonline.com/professional-programs/accessible-professional-development-resources). These are hosted events, not nine Montgomery-authored courses.
- **Modern DOE webinar with Bradley Jones and Douglas Montgomery:** [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=flqTHTbjMa8) and [downloaded JMP journal with data and analyses](downloads/jmp-companions/doe-jones-montgomery.jrn), linked by the [JMP academic library](https://www.jmp.com/en/academic/academic-webinars).
- **Related quality-control software companion:** [introductory slides](downloads/jmp-companions/ramirez-sqc.pdf) and [data/analyses ZIP](downloads/jmp-companions/isqc-data.zip) from the JMP webinar by **Brenda and José Ramírez**. These two files accompany their book about using Montgomery's text; they are not Montgomery's ASU lecture slides.

Video links were located on official source pages and the Wiley playlist metadata was inspected. Individual YouTube playback and regional availability were not tested. The ASU welcome and DOE overview clips are indexed separately from the nine guest presentations.

## Material requiring enrollment or instructor access

- **Coursera:** six Montgomery-taught or co-taught course listings were found: four in [Design of Experiments](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/design-experiments), plus *Probability, Statistical Inference and Regression Analysis* and *Classification and Planned Experiments*. Public outlines are available. The [ASU program page](https://goee.asu.edu/professional-programs/design-experiments-specialization) describes a subscription and trial; free enrollment is not evidence of full free course access. This collection does not count paid/enrolled lessons as open materials.
- **Wiley instructor resources:** the checked DOE lecture-slide links, DOE 10th-edition instructor datasets and [chapter videos](https://bcs.wiley.com/he-bcs/Books?action=resource&bcsId=11447&itemId=1119492440&resourceId=45654), and quality-control 8th-edition instructor slide/data links lead to instructor verification. No protected files were collected. This does not establish that every resource for those editions is restricted.
- **Generalized Linear Models:** publisher descriptions advertise supplementary data, but a working current public archive was not confirmed. The publisher/book links are retained in the catalogue.
- **Legacy links:** the old ASU Dropbox fireside-recordings index and an ICOTS9 proceedings link could not be retrieved. The working ASU recording links are used instead.

## Provenance and use

[catalog.json](catalog.json) contains 129 deduplicated URL records: original and landing URLs, resource type, authorship category, access observations, retrieval status, and notes. Every downloaded file has its local path, byte size, SHA-256 hash, and HTTP result. PDFs were parsed, Office file signatures checked, and ZIP/Office archives checked for corruption. No downloaded documents or scripts were executed. The 90 saved files contain 89 distinct hashes because the time-series workbook is supplied unchanged for two editions.

The [research manifests](research/) retain the discovery notes. Catalogue download results take precedence over preliminary notes about files that still needed verification. Signed temporary syllabus URL credentials were omitted; stable public viewer/API routes are recorded instead.

**Public access is not the same as an open reuse license.** No general open reuse license was identified for these third-party materials. Original copyrights and source terms remain in effect; the repository's Apache 2.0 license does not relicense the downloaded third-party content. Several ASU syllabi expressly restrict redistribution of class materials. This folder is a research collection of public files and links, not a grant of permission to publish or adapt a course from them.
