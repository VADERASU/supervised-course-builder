## Montgomery textbook references

Working classification of Douglas C. Montgomery's books in the supplied Google Scholar cited-works list. The list below groups editions, translations, and duplicate records into **12 distinct textbook titles**, with companion manuals and specialized references tracked separately.

| Textbook | Related entries and notes |
| --- | --- |
| [Design and Analysis of Experiments][design-analysis] | Includes the repeated English editions and *Diseño y análisis de experimentos*. The Minitab manual is a separate companion. |
| [Response Surface Methodology: Process and Product Optimization Using Designed Experiments][response-surface] | An advanced textbook, also used as a professional reference. |
| [Introduction to Statistical Quality Control][quality-control] | Includes *Introdução ao Controle Estatístico da Qualidade*, *Control estadístico de la calidad*, and *Pengantar pengendalian kualitas statistik*. |
| [Introduction to Linear Regression Analysis][linear-regression] | Includes *Introducción al análisis de regresión lineal* and the separate English textbook entry. The solutions manual is a supplement. |
| [Introduction to Time Series Analysis and Forecasting][intro-time-series] | By Montgomery, Jennings, and Kulahci. |
| [Engineering Statistics][engineering-statistics] | By Montgomery, Runger, and Hubele. Includes the SI version and *Estatística aplicada à engenharia*. |
| [Probability and Statistics in Engineering][probability-engineering] | The Hines–Montgomery book, with Goldsman and Borror in the fourth edition. Includes *Probabilidade e Estatística na Engenharia*. |
| [Forecasting and Time Series Analysis][forecasting] | An older textbook, distinct from *Introduction to Time Series Analysis and Forecasting*. |
| [Generalized Linear Models: With Applications in Engineering and the Sciences][generalized-linear-models] | An advanced textbook with Myers, Vining, and Robinson. |
| [Operations Research in Production Planning, Scheduling, and Inventory Control][operations-research] | The Johnson–Montgomery textbook. |
| [Applied Statistics and Probability for Engineers][applied-statistics] | The Montgomery–Runger book. Includes *Estatística Aplicada e Probabilidade para Engenheiros* and *Probabilidad y estadística aplicadas a la ingeniería*. |
| [Planejamento de Experimentos usando o Statistica][statistica] | An applied textbook by Calado and Montgomery. A distinct book, rather than a translation of *Design and Analysis of Experiments*. |

### Companion manuals, references, and chapters

- **Design and Analysis of Experiments, Minitab Manual** is a [companion manual][minitab-manual], excluded from the textbook count above.
- **Introduction to Linear Regression Analysis, 6e Solutions Manual** is a textbook supplement, excluded from the count. The underlying textbook is included above.
- **Design and Analysis of Gauge R&R Studies: Making Decisions with Confidence Intervals in Random and Mixed ANOVA Models** is classified here as a specialized reference book. Its [preface][gauge-reference] explicitly describes it as a reference.
- **Multicollinearity diagnostics** and **Experiments with a single factor: the analysis of variance** are textbook chapters, rather than separate textbooks.
- Entries with journal names, volumes, and page ranges are journal articles. The incomplete entry titled **John Wiley & Sons** cannot be classified confidently from the supplied metadata.
